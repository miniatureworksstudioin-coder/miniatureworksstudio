About the work

Built to brief, never off a shelf

Every commission is planned at scale before a single detail is added — customisation notes, reference material, and delivery timelines are agreed upfront, then tracked through to handover so nothing gets lost between the sketch and the finished piece.

Materials

Materials & finish

Most pieces start as MDF. From there, everything is hardened rather than painted.

No added colour

The brown you see comes from the hardening process itself, not paint or stain — nothing is coloured.

Longer shelf life

Hardening also toughens the material: an untreated MDF piece typically holds up 1–2 years — hardened, it lasts 4–5.

No nails

Every joint is built and held together without a single nail.

Process

From brief to finished piece

01

Brief & concept

We talk through what you want built — subject, scale, and any reference material you already have.

02

Scale planning

The piece is sketched and measured out so proportions hold up before any material is cut or shaped.

03

Build & detail

Sculpting, assembly, and the fine detailing that makes a miniature read as real at a glance.

04

Finish & handover

Final finishing, a status update with photos, and delivery — usually ahead of the agreed date.
